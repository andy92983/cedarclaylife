## Domain 1: Reels Content System

Six prompts covering the full short-form video workflow — from scroll-stopping hooks through to monetisation.

---

### 1. The Viral Hook Library

Generate 50 platform-tested hooks across every psychological trigger type.


## Domain 2: Solopreneur Cash Flow System

Six prompts that build a complete cash flow operating system — from predicting the next crisis through to preventing it permanently. Run them in order for the full system, or use individually as needed.

---

### 1. Predict Your Cash Flow Crisis

Identify the warning signals 60–90 days before a crisis forces a bad decision.

```
Act as a forensic cash flow analyst who reads every pattern in a solopreneur's revenue data that historically precedes a cash crisis.

Task: Analyse my solopreneur revenue patterns and predict exactly where and when a cash crisis will hit — before it forces me to take on a bad client, drop my prices in panic, or make a desperate decision that costs more than the crisis itself.

Before starting, ask me for:
- Monthly revenue for the last 6 months
- Monthly fixed costs
- Client payment terms
- Any upcoming large expenses

Then deliver:
1. Revenue Pattern Map — every month where payments dropped, why, and whether those conditions exist now
2. Expense Pattern Map — every fixed cost that continues regardless of client revenue, and how many days of zero new clients my current cash can sustain
3. Crisis Trigger Points — the specific dollar amounts that push cash flow negative
4. Cash Survival Days — exactly how many days away a crisis is if current patterns continue unchanged
5. Weekly Early Warning Signals — checkable in under 10 minutes without an accountant

Rules:
- Use 6 months minimum of revenue data
- Crisis trigger points must be specific dollar amounts
- Cash survival must use current cash position — never projected payments
- Timeline must be expressed in specific days
- Test: would monitoring these signals weekly show a cash crisis coming 60 days before it arrived?
```

---

### 2. Find Your Revenue Gaps

Map every predictable income hole and build the bridge before it opens.

```
Act as a solopreneur revenue gap analyst who identifies every predictable hole in a one-person business income calendar.

Task: Map every predictable revenue gap in my solopreneur business and build the exact strategy that fills each gap before it opens.

Before starting, ask me for:
- Monthly revenue for the last 12 months
- Typical project or contract length
- Primary client acquisition channel

Then deliver:
1. Monthly Revenue Map — every month where revenue dropped below my expense baseline
2. Gap Pattern — is the drop seasonal, tied to project completion cycles, or outreach gaps
3. Gap Dollar Amount — the exact dollar shortfall per identified gap
4. Revenue Bridge Strategy — the specific outreach campaign, limited offer, or existing client upsell that fills each gap if activated 60 days before it opens
5. 12-Month Gap Prevention Calendar — the specific action to take 60 days before each gap arrives

Rules:
- Use 12 months of data — seasonal patterns are invisible in less
- Every gap must have a specific dollar amount attached
- Revenue bridge must use current audience and existing offers only
- 60-day lead time is non-negotiable
- Test: if every bridge action activated on schedule, would any month fall below the expense baseline?
```

---

### 3. Calculate Your Cash Runway

Know exactly how many days your business survives with zero new client payments.

```
Act as a solopreneur cash runway specialist.

Task: Calculate my exact cash runway and build the specific expense and revenue moves that extend it to a safe level before I ever need it.

Before starting, ask me for:
- Current cash position
- Monthly fixed costs
- Monthly variable costs
- Current monthly client revenue

Then deliver:
1. Burn Rate — exactly how much cash leaves every month regardless of new client work
2. Current Runway in Days — exactly how many days current cash lasts at burn rate with zero new client payments
3. Three Fastest Expense Reductions — cuts that extend runway without touching client acquisition activities
4. Three Fastest Revenue Moves — executable within 7 days using existing relationships and offers
5. 90-Day Runway Extension Plan — the specific cuts and revenue moves that push runway to a minimum of 90 days within the next 30 days

Rules:
- Burn rate must include every fixed cost including annual subscriptions divided by 12
- Runway assumes zero new revenue — conservative assumptions prevent false security
- Expense reductions must never cut client acquisition activities
- Revenue moves must use existing relationships and offers only — no building from scratch
- Test: if every runway extension move implemented today, could the business survive 90 days of zero new client payments?
```

---

### 4. Audit Your Payment Terms

Calculate the exact cash flow damage your current terms are creating and close the gap permanently.

```
Act as a solopreneur cash flow timing specialist who calculates exactly how much damage a one-person business's payment terms are creating.

Task: Audit every client payment term and calculate the exact cash flow damage the timing gap is creating — then build the specific changes that close it permanently without losing a single client.

Before starting, ask me for:
- Current client payment terms
- Average days to payment
- Typical invoice amounts
- Fixed expenses that fall due while waiting for client payments

Then deliver:
1. Timing Gap Per Client Type — exactly how many days pass between delivering work and receiving payment for each client type
2. Cash Flow Damage — the specific dollar amount of monthly expenses that fall due during the average timing gap
3. Restructuring Opportunities — every client relationship where terms could move toward upfront deposits, milestone payments, or retainers
4. New Payment Term Structure — specific deposit percentages, milestone triggers, and retainer amounts that eliminate the gap entirely
5. Client Conversation Script — the exact words to use with existing clients when presenting new terms without triggering a cancellation

Rules:
- Timing gap must be calculated per client type
- Cash flow damage must be a specific dollar amount
- Every existing client must be assessed — never assume refusal before the conversation
- New structure must eliminate the gap entirely, not reduce it by half
- Test: if every client moved to the new structure, would the timing gap reach zero days?
```

### 5. Build Your Cash Reserve System

Create an automatic 90-day survival buffer from current revenue.

```
Act as a solopreneur cash reserve architect who builds automatic savings systems.

Task: Build a complete automatic cash reserve system that accumulates a 90-day business survival buffer from current revenue — without requiring more clients, higher prices, or willpower.

Before starting, ask me for:
- Monthly business expenses
- Current monthly revenue
- Existing emergency savings
- Current automatic savings habits

Then deliver:
1. Target Reserve — exactly how much cash covers 90 days of solopreneur business expenses with zero new client revenue
2. Current Reserve Gap — the specific dollar difference between existing savings and the 90-day target
3. Automatic Accumulation System — the specific percentage of every client payment that transfers to a separate reserve account the moment it is received, before entering the operating account
4. Timeline in Months — exactly how many months at the accumulation rate until the 90-day target is fully funded
5. Reserve Protection Rules — the specific conditions under which the reserve can be accessed and the exact rebuild protocol to follow the moment any portion is used

Rules:
- Target reserve calculated from actual monthly expenses — never aspirational or reduced
- Automatic transfer happens at payment receipt — never after any spending decision
- Accumulation percentage must work at current revenue
- Timeline must be a specific number of months
- Protection rules must be written before they are needed — crisis-time decisions are always wrong
- Test: if implemented today, would the reserve reach its 90-day target within the calculated timeline without additional client revenue?
```

## Domain 3: AI Agency Building

Seven prompts covering the complete AI agency workflow — from niche selection through to weekly CEO reporting.

---

### 1. The Niche Domination Prompt

Identify which industry to target before building anything.

```
I am going to paste 10 industries below. For each one, answer:

1. How much does this industry currently spend on manual tasks that AI tools under $100/month could handle?
2. What is the realistic annual saving per business if those tasks were automated?
3. Who is the decision-maker (title, not department)?
4. How easy is it to reach that decision-maker cold?

Rank the 10 industries by total opportunity (savings × number of businesses × reachability).

Industries: [paste your 10 industries here]
```

---

### 2. The Offer Builder Prompt

Structure a three-tier offer before the first sales call.

```
I help [niche] businesses save money by installing AI tools that handle [specific function].

Build me a three-tier offer structure:
- Tier 1: $2,000 quick-win install
- Tier 2: $5,000 full setup
- Tier 3: $10,000/month retainer

For each tier include:
- Exactly what is delivered
- How long it takes to deliver
- The specific AI tools used
- The business outcome the client can measure

Make each tier feel like a natural upgrade from the previous one.
```

---

### 3. The Cold Email Machine Prompt

Generate five tested cold email variations for outreach.

```
Write me 5 cold email variations for [niche] business owners.

Requirements for every email:
- Lead with the cost of their current manual process (use a specific number or estimate)
- Offer to cut that cost by 60–80% using AI
- Keep each email under 90 words
- No marketing language — sound like a real person, not a copywriter
- End with one low-friction CTA (a 15-minute call, not a demo request)

Label each variation with the psychological angle it uses (e.g. loss aversion, social proof, curiosity).
```

---

### 4. The Objection Destroyer Prompt

Pre-load responses to every common sales call objection.

```
Here are the 5 most common objections I hear on sales calls:

[List your 5 objections here]

For each objection:
1. Write a 2-sentence response that reframes using a specific number or mini case study
2. Identify the underlying fear driving the objection
3. Give me one follow-up question that moves the conversation forward after I deliver the reframe

Keep every response conversational — this is a live call, not an email.
```

---

### 5. The Onboarding Playbook Prompt

Build a repeatable client onboarding checklist for every new install.

```
Build a step-by-step client onboarding checklist for installing [specific AI tool] for a [niche] business.

Structure it in three phases:
- Pre-install (what I need from the client before I start)
- Day 1 (what I set up and in what order)
- Day 7 (what the client can see, measure, and report to their team)

For each step include:
- Who does it (me or the client)
- How long it takes
- What success looks like

Format as a checklist I can duplicate for every new client.
```

---

### 6. The Upsell Identifier Prompt

Find the next three tools to offer every installed client.

```
I just installed [specific AI tool] for a [niche] client.

Based on their business type, identify 3 other AI tools I could offer that would save them money or increase revenue.

For each tool include:
- The tool name and monthly cost
- What manual process it replaces
- The estimated monthly saving or revenue increase for this client
- The natural conversation hook — how do I bring it up without sounding like I am just upselling

Rank by fastest ROI for the client.
```

---

### 7. The Weekly CEO Report Prompt

Analyse your pipeline numbers and focus the next week.

```
Here are my numbers this week:

- Leads generated: [X]
- Calls booked: [X]
- Deals closed: [X]
- Revenue: [$X]
- Client installs completed: [X]

Analyse my pipeline. Tell me:
1. Where I am leaking money or losing deals
2. The single highest-leverage action for next week based on these numbers
3. One metric I am not tracking that I should be
4. Whether my close rate is healthy for this stage of the business

Be direct. No encouragement — just diagnosis and next action.
```
